KPI Definitions

Denial rate

Formula:

Denied claims divided by total submitted claims.

Target:

Below 8 percent.

Escalation threshold:

Above 10 percent for 2 consecutive weeks.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows how often claims are failing after submission.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- eligibility errors
- missing prior authorization
- documentation gaps
- coding mismatch
- payer-specific rule failure

Clean claim rate

Formula:

Clean claims divided by total submitted claims.

Target:

90 percent or higher.

Escalation threshold:

Below 85 percent for 2 consecutive weeks.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows how often claims leave the organization correctly the first time.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- registration defects
- missing insurance verification
- incomplete documentation
- incorrect modifiers
- claim edit failures

Revenue at risk

Formula:

Sum of denied claim dollars still unresolved.

Target:

Declining weekly.

Escalation threshold:

Any Tier 1 unresolved amount above $2,000 or any claim near timely filing risk.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows how much reimbursement is exposed to delay or loss.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- high-dollar claims stuck without owner action
- delayed payer response
- missing documentation
- unresolved coding review

Average denial age

Formula:

Total denial age in days divided by number of active denials.

Target:

Below 14 days.

Escalation threshold:

Above 20 days.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows how long denials stay unresolved.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- unclear owner assignment
- delayed follow-up
- missing documentation
- payer response delays

Tier 1 queue volume

Formula:

Count of high-risk denial items.

Target:

Low and actively worked daily.

Escalation threshold:

Any Tier 1 item without owner action within 24 hours.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows which claims need immediate attention.

Tier 1 includes:

- high-dollar claims
- timely filing risk
- active escalations
- repeated payer rejection
- coding or authorization holds blocking payment

Rework rate

Formula:

Claims requiring repeat correction divided by total corrected claims.

Target:

Below 10 percent.

Escalation threshold:

Above 15 percent.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows whether corrections are solving the issue or creating repeat work.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- incomplete first correction
- unclear denial reason
- poor documentation review
- repeated routing errors

Ownership gap count

Formula:

Count of active denial items without clear owner or next action.

Target:

Zero.

Escalation threshold:

Any item over 48 hours without assigned owner.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows where work is sitting without accountability.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- unclear workflow rules
- informal handoffs
- queue overload
- role confusion

Escalation count

Formula:

Count of denial items escalated to supervisor, manager, or cross-functional owner.

Target:

Stable or decreasing.

Escalation threshold:

Increase of 20 percent week over week.

Plain-language meaning:

This shows where normal workflow is no longer enough to resolve the issue.

Upstream causes when degraded:

- authorization breakdowns
- documentation delays
- payer policy mismatch
- unresolved ownership
